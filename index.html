How It Happened

It wasn't just a golf trip. It's our story.

Nine days of historic links golf, a celebrated St Andrews pub, the Home of Golf on foot, and father-son memories that will last forever. The plan changed as we went — we skipped the Highlands, stayed in Fife, and played more golf than we ever booked. This is the record of what actually happened.
