⛳ ST ANDREWS COURSE COMPARISON
"Beyond the Old Course"

🏆 Castle Course - RECOMMENDED

Price: £22.50 promotional (normally £180)
Character: Spectacular clifftop views, modern links masterpiece
Designer: David McLay Kidd (2008)
Why Play: Best value for premium experience

💪 Jubilee Course

Price: £18.75 promotional (normally £150)
Character: Most difficult St Andrews course, narrow fairways
Why Play: Locals' favorite challenge, true test of links golf

📜 New Course

Price: Higher than promotional courses
Character: Designed by Old Tom Morris (1895)
Why Play: Many consider it better than Old Course for average golfers

🌱 Eden Course

Price: £9.50 promotional (normally £75)
Character: Shortest, easiest, most forgiving
Why Play: Great warm-up round, excellent value
