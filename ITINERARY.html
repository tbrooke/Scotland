⛳ ST ANDREWS COURSE COMPARISON
"All the Links — Context for Your Visit"

🏔️ Castle Course — YOUR COURSE ✅ CONFIRMED Fri Jul 3

Character: Dramatic clifftop links overlooking the town and Old Course
Designer: David McLay Kidd (2008) — same architect as Bandon Dunes
Why Play: Spectacular and unique — nothing else in Scotland looks like this

🏆 Old Course

Character: The home of golf since 1552 — history on every hole
This Trip: Walk the fairways Saturday July 4 (open to public) — Swilcan Bridge, 18th hole
Fun fact: Closed every Sunday by tradition — the links becomes a public park

💪 Jubilee Course

Character: Most difficult St Andrews course, narrow fairways
Why Play: Locals' favourite challenge, true test of links golf

📜 New Course

Character: Designed by Old Tom Morris (1895)
Why Play: Many consider it better than Old Course for average golfers

🌱 Eden Course

Character: Shortest, easiest, most forgiving
Why Play: Great warm-up round, excellent value
