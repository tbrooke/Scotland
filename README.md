**A Father & Sons Journey to the Home of Golf**

## What It Was

- **Who:** Dad (74) & Twin Sons (38)
- **When:** June 29 – July 7, 2026 (9 days)
- **Where:** East Lothian & the Kingdom of Fife
- **Golf:** 8 rounds across 6 courses — the twins played all 8, Dad played 6

> We planned a Highland finish at Nairn. Instead we dropped the whole northern leg, stayed in Fife, and played more golf than we ever booked — Kingsbarns twice, the Golf House Club at Elie, and a 36-hole Gullane day from the twins.

## What Changed From the Plan

The trip was originally booked to finish in the Highlands: two nights at 51 Society Street in Nairn, a round at Nairn Dunbar, and a visit to Glenfarclas Distillery. We dropped the entire northern leg and stayed in Fife instead — trading the long drive north for more links golf (Kingsbarns twice and Elie) and a much gentler final morning to the airport.
