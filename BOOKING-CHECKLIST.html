🔀 What Changed From the Plan

The trip was booked to finish in the Highlands — two nights in Nairn, a round at Nairn Dunbar, and Glenfarclas Distillery. We dropped the whole northern leg and stayed in Fife instead.

In its place: a night at The Inn at Kingsbarns, a third night in St Andrews, and three extra rounds of golf — Kingsbarns twice and the Golf House Club at Elie. It also cut out the two longest drives (St Andrews→Nairn and the pre-dawn Nairn→Edinburgh run), leaving a gentle final morning to the airport.
