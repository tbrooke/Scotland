📺 What We Watched

THE UN/OFFICIAL GUIDE TO GOLF IN SCOTLAND

Erik Anders Lang & The Random Golf Club

Erik Anders Lang and The Random Golf Club spent several weeks playing Scotland's greatest links courses and filming every round. The result is one of the best golf travel series ever made — honest, funny, genuinely in love with the game. No corporate sponsors, no perfect swing tips. Just real golfers on real courses.

These are the episodes we watched before we left — matched to the courses and regions on our trip. Watching them on the flight over meant we arrived already knowing what the courses looked and felt like. Episode 7, the Kingsbarns round, turned out to be especially fitting: we ended up playing Kingsbarns twice.

Erik Anders Lang on Dunbar Golf Club: "This might be the Pebble Beach of Scotland." It was our first golf of the trip, on Day 3.

⛳ East Lothian — Where We Started

Days 3–4 · Dunbar (Wed Jul 1) · Gullane (Thu Jul 2)

We opened the trip on East Lothian's Golf Coast — one of the most concentrated stretches of links golf anywhere in the world. These two episodes cover the courses and coastline we spent Days 3 and 4 on: Dunbar, and Gullane (where the twins backed up #1 with #2 for a 36-hole day).

Episode 4

The Pebble Beach of Scotland

⏰ Watch before Day 3 (Wed Jul 1) — Dunbar Golf Club is your first round. EAL coins the phrase right here. → Day 3 in Itinerary

What to watch for: The approach from town, the view from the first tee out over the Firth of Forth, and the way the course hugs the sea wall for holes that feel genuinely unlike anywhere else. This is where Erik says the words — and you'll understand exactly why when you're standing on the 1st tee. Also notice how walkable and friendly the club is. You'll be there in six weeks.

Episode 5

Scotland's Golf Coast W A Duck

⏰ Watch before Day 4 (Thu Jul 2) — Gullane Golf Club #1, your first championship links. → Day 4 in Itinerary

What to watch for: The East Lothian landscape — heathland turning to links, the Firth of Forth always somewhere on the horizon, the quiet village feel of Gullane. This episode captures the atmosphere of the Golf Coast better than any travel piece you'll find. The duck has strong opinions about golf and will remind you of someone you know.

🏴󠁧󠁢󠁳󠁣󠁴󠁿 Fife & St Andrews — The Home of Golf

Days 6–8 · Sat Jul 4 – Mon Jul 6

Fife is where the trip really opened up. We based ourselves in St Andrews for three nights and played Kingsbarns (twice), Dumbarnie, and the Golf House Club at Elie, walked the Old Course after losing the ballot, and played the Himalayas putting green. These episodes cover the courses, coastline, and atmosphere of the Fife peninsula we spent the back half of the trip in.

Episode 6

Skipped School for the 11th Oldest Course in the World

⏰ Watch on arrival in St Andrews (Sat Jul 4 evening) — sets the Fife coastal mood. Leven Links is a short drive from Dumbarnie. → Days 4–6 in Itinerary

Episode 6 covers Leven Links — one of the oldest clubs in Fife, just a few miles from Dumbarnie Links where you're playing Sunday. It's the regional flavour episode: the East Neuk fishing villages, the Fife coastline character, the sense of history that saturates every old links course in this corner of Scotland. Watch it on the playlist — it's right after Episode 5.

▶ Watch Episode 6 on YouTube

What to watch for: The East Neuk atmosphere — the stone walls, the sea wind, the courses that look like they grew out of the ground rather than being designed. This is the mood you'll be in for Days 4–6.

Episode 7

Magical Round at Kingsbarns

⏰ The one that mattered most. We ended up playing Kingsbarns twice — the twins at 7:30 AM on the 4th, then all three of us on the 6th (Dad with caddy Gary). This episode is the course we came back for. → Days 6 & 8 in the Log

What to watch for: The Firth of Forth from the Fife side — the same views you'll have at Dumbarnie, which is right down the road. Kingsbarns and Dumbarnie share the same dramatic clifftop-and-beach DNA. Notice how the course plays into and across the wind from the sea — that's exactly what you'll face Sunday morning at 8:50 AM. Erik's joy here is infectious. It'll get you ready.

Episode 8

We Surprised Stuart — Twice — at The Old Course, St Andrews

⏰ We lost the ballot — both times. No tee time on the Old Course. But we walked it on foot on the 4th of July — Swilcan Bridge, the 18th, the hallowed turf — and this episode captures exactly what that ground feels like underfoot. → Day 6 in the Log

What to watch for: The whole thing — but specifically the first tee, the walk down 18, and the Swilcan Bridge moment. Erik captures what it actually feels like to stand on that course for the first time — the weight of it, the history underfoot. Notice the road behind the 17th green. Notice Hell Bunker yawning in the middle of the 14th. Notice how the town is right there, houses lining the edges of the fairways. We didn't get to play it — the ballot beat us twice — but we walked every inch of that closing stretch, and this episode is the next best thing to a tee time.
